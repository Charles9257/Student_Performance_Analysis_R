# Student_Performance_Analysis_R
This study analyses the factors influencing student academic performance in higher education. The study focuses on demographic, socioeconomic, and institutional factors affecting student outcomes

#Problem Statement
What are the significant factors affecting student academic performance in higher education based on demographic and institutional variables?
Examining the demographic and institutional influences on student academic performance in higher education offers valuable insights into the challenges students face. Drawing from the perspectives of Tinto (1993) and Pascarella & Terenzini (2005), “this study will support data-driven approaches to enhancing student success and creating a more inclusive and supportive learning environment”.
